PSYCHOLOGY EXPERIMENT

Memory & Encoding

A short study of how different strategies affect free recall.

Welcome

You will study 18 words in three blocks. Each block uses a different encoding strategy:

  • Maintenance rehearsal: repeat the word silently to yourself.
  • Self-reference: think about how the word relates to you or your life.
  • Mental imagery: form a clear mental picture of the word.

The order of strategies and word lists is randomized. Each word appears for 4 seconds. After studying, you will complete a 30-second counting task and then recall as many words as you can.

For the best results, complete the study without taking notes or looking away to search for the words.
